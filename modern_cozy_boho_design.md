# Modern Cozy Boho: Bedroom & Living Space Design Proposal

## Overview
This design transforms your 22.7 sq meter WG room into a multifunctional, warm, and inviting retreat. The Modern Cozy Boho style focuses on earthy tones, natural textures, and layered textiles to create a space that feels deeply personal and relaxing. We have carefully zoned the room into distinct areas for sleeping, working, living/socializing, and storage, while ensuring the inward-swinging balcony door remains completely unobstructed.

## Color Palette & Materials
* **Base Colors:** Cream, warm white, and soft beige to keep the space bright.
* **Accent Colors:** Terracotta, mustard yellow, and sage green for warmth and depth.
* **Materials:** Natural woods (light to medium tone), rattan or cane webbing, textured fabrics (bouclé, linen, macrame), and matte brass or black metal accents.
* **Decor Element:** Abundant indoor plants (e.g., Monstera, Pothos, Snake Plant) to bring life and vibrant color to the space.

## Functional Zoning & Layout Strategy

Given the location of the main door and the balcony door on opposite/adjacent walls, we divide the room into three primary zones to maximize flow:

### 1. The Sleep & Storage Zone
* **Location:** Tucked into the quietest corner, likely near the entrance but away from the direct walkway to the balcony.
* **Bed (120x200cm):** A warm wooden or upholstered platform bed. To save space, it can be pushed against one wall or positioned in a cozy corner. We'll layer it with a textured duvet, a chunky knit throw, and an assortment of pillows in terracotta and cream.
* **Wardrobe (Medium):** Placed along the wall adjacent to the bed or near the entrance. A wooden wardrobe with woven rattan panels on the doors would perfectly fit the Boho aesthetic.
* **Small Storage:** A low wooden dresser or a set of rattan baskets tucked under the bed or beside the wardrobe for extra linens or folded clothes.

### 2. The Living & Socializing Zone
* **Location:** The center of the room or slightly offset towards the window/balcony wall (but out of the door's swing path). This acts as a buffer between the sleep and work areas.
* **Sofa:** A small, plush loveseat or a comfortable 2-seater sofa in a warm tone (like mustard or soft terracotta).
* **TV & Media Console:** A low-profile, natural wood media console placed opposite the sofa.
* **Boho Touches:** A large, patterned Persian-style or jute rug anchors this space. A small, organic-shaped wooden coffee table and a textured floor pouf add to the relaxed, social vibe.

### 3. The Work Zone
* **Location:** Positioned near the window/balcony to maximize natural light during the day, but situated parallel to the balcony door to ensure the swing path is 100% clear.
* **Desk:** A spacious natural wood desk (large enough for a laptop + 2 monitors side-by-side).
* **Chair:** An ergonomic office chair that doesn't sacrifice style—perhaps one with a warm fabric or leather-look finish.
* **Lighting:** A stylish brass or matte black desk lamp, and a tall floor plant next to the desk to keep the workspace feeling fresh and inspiring.

## Lighting & Decor Recommendations
* **Lighting:** Layered lighting is key. Use warm white bulbs (2700K). Add a textured pendant light (like woven rattan), a warm floor lamp in the living area, and bedside reading lights.
* **Textiles:** Layer a soft, textured throw on the sofa. Hang sheer, light-filtering linen curtains on the windows to soften the natural light.
* **Wall Decor:** A gallery wall above the sofa or bed featuring a mix of framed art, woven wall hangings, and small floating shelves for trailing plants.

## Crucial Constraint Kept:
* **Balcony Flow:** The central walkway from the main door to the balcony is kept wide and clear. The sofa and desk are intentionally arranged so that the inward-swinging balcony door can be opened fully without hitting any furniture.
